Why I built FriendMap

Your people are out there. They're just hard to find.

We're more connected online than ever, but finding those same people nearby is still surprisingly hard.

FriendMap helps you discover local communities and start real conversations nearby, then takes the awkwardness out of organising, so those conversations naturally turn into real-world meetups.
