Why I built this

Your people are out there. They're just hard to find.

We're more connected online than ever — but finding those same people nearby is still surprisingly hard.

FriendMap helps you discover local communities and start real conversations nearby — then takes the awkwardness out of organising, so those conversations naturally turn into real-world meetups.

How it works

1

Discover

Browse local communities on a live map and find people nearby who share your interests.

2

Join & Chat

Join a community, chat with members, or create your own and grow it locally.

3

FriendMap Suggests

Once a community reaches 3 members, FriendMap automatically suggests a simple meetup — like coffee, a walk, or something that fits the community.

4

Vote Together

Everyone votes on the day, time and location. No one has to awkwardly organise everything themselves.

5

Meet Up

On meetup day, let everyone know you're on your way or have arrived — making it easy for the whole group to find each other.
