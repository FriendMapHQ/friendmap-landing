Why I built this

Your people are out there. They're just hard to find.

We're more connected online than ever — but finding those same people nearby is still surprisingly hard.

FriendMap helps you discover communities and shared interests right where you live, so local connection feels as easy as opening a map.

Founder · Built in Ireland 🇮🇪
