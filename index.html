Why I built this

Your people are out there. They're just hard to find.

We're more connected online than ever — but finding those same people nearby is still surprisingly hard.

FriendMap helps you discover local communities and start real conversations nearby — then takes the awkwardness out of organising, so those conversations naturally turn into real-world meetups.

How it works

1

Discover

Browse local communities on a live map and find people nearby who share your interests.

2

Create Your Own

Can't find a community that interests you? You can create your own by long-pressing anywhere on the map.

3

FriendMap Suggests

Once a community reaches 3 members, FriendMap automatically suggests a casual meetup — like coffee, a walk, or something that fits the group. Nobody has to organise a thing.

4

Vote Together

Everyone votes on the day, time, and a nearby location. The best option wins — no awkward back and forth, no group chat chaos.

5

Meet Up

Once voting is done, FriendMap confirms the meetup automatically — day, time and place, all locked in. Just show up.

On the day of the meetup

Everyone can share their live status so nobody wonders who's coming.

Members tap to update whether they haven't left yet, are on the way, or have already arrived — so the whole group knows what to expect before anyone walks in the door.

After the meetup

Meetups stay in the community history so everyone can look back and look forward to the next one.

The whole journey — suggestion, votes, meetup — lives in the community chat. A new suggestion is automatically posted the following week, so the momentum never stops.
