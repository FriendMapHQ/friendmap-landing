How it works

From finding a community to meeting up, in five steps.

Step 1

Discover

Browse local communities on a live map and find people nearby who share your interests.

Step 2

Create your own

Can't find a community that interests you? Create your own by long-pressing anywhere on the map.

Step 3

FriendMap suggests

Once a community reaches 3 members, FriendMap suggests a casual meetup, like coffee, a walk, or something that fits the group. Nobody has to organise a thing.

Step 4

Vote together

Everyone votes on the day, time and a nearby place. The best option wins: no awkward back and forth, no group chat chaos.

Step 5

Meet up

Once voting is done, FriendMap confirms the meetup: day, time and place, all locked in. Just show up.

The meetup

Before, during and after.

On the day

Know who's coming

Everyone can share whether they haven't left yet, are on the way, or have already arrived, so the whole group knows what to expect before anyone walks in the door.

After the meetup

Keep the momentum

The whole journey, from suggestion to votes to meetup, lives in the community chat. A new suggestion is posted the following week, so the momentum never stops.
