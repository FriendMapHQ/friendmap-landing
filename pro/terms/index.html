These terms apply when you sign up for FriendMap Pro, along with the FriendMap Terms of Service. By signing up, you agree to them.

Who Pro is for

Pro is for businesses and organisations that run a venue. By signing up, you confirm you're acting for a business or organisation, not as a private consumer. Each Pro account runs one venue.

Price and billing

Pro costs €49 a month. It renews automatically each month, and is charged to the card you provide, until you cancel.

If we change the price, we'll email you at least 30 days before it applies to you, so you have time to cancel.

Free first month

New venues get their first month free, once per account. You add your card when you sign up, and the first charge is taken when the free month ends. If you cancel before then, you're not charged.

Payments through Stripe

Payments are processed by Stripe. You enter your card details on Stripe's secure page, and FriendMap never sees or stores your full card number. Stripe sends your receipts. Stripe handles your payment details as described in its Privacy Policy.

If a payment fails, Stripe may try your card again. If it still can't be collected, your subscription can end and Pro will switch off.

Cancelling

There's no contract, and you can cancel any time from the Pro page. Pro stays on until the end of the month you've paid for, then switches off. Your community and its members stay, and it goes back to being a normal community.

Deleting your FriendMap account cancels your subscription straight away, and any time left in that month ends with it.

Refunds

We don't normally refund part of a month after you cancel. If something has gone wrong, such as a charge you didn't expect, email us and we'll look into it. Nothing here affects any rights you have under the law.

Changes and ending Pro

We may change Pro's features over time. If we make a change that significantly affects you, or decide to stop offering Pro, we'll email you in advance. We may end Pro for accounts that break these terms or the Terms of Service.
